The source checkpoint was converted to MLX safetensors with demucs-mlx 1.4.6
and MLX 0.31.2. The safetensors format is data-only;
Wemute verifies the outer package SHA-256 and the inner safetensors SHA-256
recorded by htdemucs_config.json before loading it.

Wemute additionally validated the converted model against the official
PyTorch HTDemucs output and requires a successful two-stem audio smoke test
before publishing this package.
